# Concept artwork and high-level requirements

The two retained JPG files in this directory are visual concepts, not dimensioned CAD designs. Their current proposed requirements are indexed in [the Draw.io baseline](../CAS/Drawio/README.md). The protected-truck concept and its ARM requirements moved to [jfxai4rffs](https://github.com/robotics-intelligent-systems/jfxai4rffs/blob/main/docs/requirements/emergency-response-support-concepts.md) on 13 October 2026. Every current image maps to one concept-specific diagram plus the common requirements diagram.

Do not infer dimensions, protection level, endurance, propulsion technology, certification or implemented autonomy from artwork. Numerical parameters are open engineering decisions tracked in each diagram.
